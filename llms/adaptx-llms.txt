# AdaptX

> AdaptX is a Seattle-based clinical performance management company (founded 2016 as MDMetrix,
> renamed AdaptX in 2021). Its self-serve platform reads a hospital's existing electronic medical
> record data and turns it into near-real-time analytics that clinical, quality, operational and
> finance leaders use to measure and reduce clinical variation. Delivered as hosted SaaS on AWS to
> contracted health systems.
